# Team Allocation

A Bolt is one build pass over a piece of the work, ending in something that runs. A mob is a group that works on one thing together; here the "mob" is the AI developer agent working with you. Based on `bolt-plan.md` and the answers in `delivery-planning-questions.md`.

No team formation stage ran for this plan (it is not part of the mvp plan), and you are a solo builder who decides alone. [team practices, Q5 intent capture] So every Bolt is built by the AI developer agent, and you review and approve each one.

| Bolt | Unit | Built by | Reviewed and approved by |
|------|------|----------|--------------------------|
| B1 | u1-core-check | AI developer agent | You (product owner and builder) |
| B2 | u5-quality-measurement | AI developer agent | You |
| B3 | u2-page-warnings | AI developer agent | You |
| B4 | u4-openjev-source | AI developer agent | You |
| B5 | u3-verdict-memory | AI developer agent | You |

## Notes

- Bolts run one at a time. [Q4]
- Because there is one person on the project, there is no second-person review of code. [team practices]
- The checks that must be done by a person are the integrated demos at the end of B1, B3 and B4, and reading the quality report at the end of B2.
- There is only one team, so this is not a program board across several teams.

## Assumptions & Open Questions
