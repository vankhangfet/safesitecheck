# User Stories — Story Plan and Questions

The stories will follow the "As a [person], I want [goal], so that [benefit]" format, check each story against the INVEST list (independent, negotiable, valuable, estimable, small, testable), carry Given/When/Then criteria, and get a priority (Must, Should, Could or Won't).

## Q1. [Product owner] Which kinds of people should the stories be written for?

Why we ask: every story names the person who benefits, and the requirements mention three roles: someone browsing, someone setting up the extension, and someone measuring detection quality.

- A. Three personas: an everyday employee, the person who sets up the extension, and the product owner who measures detection quality
- B. Two personas: an everyday employee, and one person who both sets up the extension and measures quality (you)
- C. One persona: the everyday employee only
- D. Not yet defined
- X. Other (please specify)

[Answer]: A. Three personas: an everyday employee, the person who sets up the extension, and the product owner who measures detection quality

## Q2. [Product owner] How should the stories be grouped?

Why we ask: the grouping decides how stories are read and later split into pieces of work.

- A. By the person's journey: open a page, see the verdict, respond to a warning, set up
- B. By feature area, following the requirements: checking, showing the verdict, warnings, could not check, settings, sources, measurement
- C. By persona
- D. Not yet defined
- X. Other (please specify)

[Answer]: B. By feature area, following the requirements: checking, showing the verdict, warnings, could not check, settings, sources, measurement

## Q3. [Product owner] How big should each story be?

Why we ask: smaller stories are easier to build and test one at a time, but there will be more of them.

- A. Small: about one to three days each, with three to six criteria
- B. Larger: one story per feature area with more criteria each
- C. Not yet defined
- X. Other (please specify)

[Answer]: A. Small: about one to three days each, with three to six criteria
